Safety Instructions

Actionable Protocols & Guidelines
Emergency First Response If you feel in immediate danger, press and hold the SOS button or call 112 directly. Do not hesitate.
1 Pre-Journey Preparation
  • Share your destination via the Journey tab to activate 50m corridor monitoring.
  • Keep your phone battery above 20% and location services (GPS) turned ON.
2 In Public / En Route
  • Stay on well-lit main streets; avoid poorly lit shortcuts or isolated alleyways.
  • If followed or uneasy, use Fake Call to simulate an active conversation while heading towards a crowded shop or public establishment.
3 Incident & Evidence
  • Use Capture Evidence to record encrypted photos/audio without leaving traces on device storage.
  • Keep emergency contacts updated with at least 2 family members or trusted friends.
