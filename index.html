Safety Instructions

Immediate Emergency Response

If you are in immediate threat or physical danger, call 112 directly or trigger Emergency SOS.

Call 112 Directly
ACTIONABLE SAFETY PROTOCOLS
1 Pre-Journey Preparation
  • Share your destination via the Journey tab to activate 50m corridor monitoring.
  • Keep your phone battery above 20% and device Location (GPS) set to High Accuracy.
  • Ensure at least 2 family members or trusted guardians are linked in Emergency Contacts.
2 In Public & En Route
  • Stay on well-lit main streets; avoid poorly lit shortcuts or isolated alleyways.
  • If followed or feeling uneasy, use Fake Call to simulate an active conversation while heading towards a crowded shop or public establishment.
  • Hold your phone ready with quick access to the power button or SOS trigger.
3 Public Transport & Cabs
  • Cross-check vehicle and driver registration details before getting into auto/cab.
  • In buses, choose seats near the driver, conductor, or other women commuters.
  • Alert passengers and driver loudly at any sign of harassment or suspicious route changes.
4 Incident & Evidence Capture
  • Use Capture Evidence to record encrypted photos/audio without leaving gallery traces.
  • Files are automatically geotagged with exact GPS coordinates and server timestamp.
5 Critical Defense Principles
  • Trust your intuition: If a situation feels wrong, leave immediately without hesitating.
  • Target vulnerable areas (eyes, throat, groin) if physical escape requires sudden defense.
